Bouquet math

The market stall sells flowers by the bunch, the recipe lives in a florist's head, and nobody knows what a day of vase life actually costs. Bouquet math splits a stem count into focal, secondary and filler on the classic recipe, tells you how many bunches to buy and how many stems come home extra, and turns labeled vase lives into a cost per day on the table. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Stem plan - a total stem count and the labeled recipe become focal, secondary and filler counts, plus the cut length for your vase.
  • Bunch plan - stems needed against bunch size and price become bunches to buy, leftovers and the counter total.
  • Life plan - labeled vase lives by stem type average into days of enjoyment and cost per day.

Labeled norms

Labeled recipe: 3:5:8 focal, secondary, filler - the classic floristry proportion. Labeled cut: stems about 1.5 times the vase height. Labeled vase lives: 7 days focal, 10 secondary, 14 filler - illustrative estimates, since variety, season and water changes move them. The florist's eye and the flower's own condition win.
